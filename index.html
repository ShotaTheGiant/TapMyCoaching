About the coach

🇺🇦 🇧🇬

Mykhailo Volkov is an active MMA fighter, a purple belt in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu and a decorated competitor across several combat disciplines, from the cage to the mats of Europe’s biggest jiu-jitsu and grappling events.

Everything he teaches comes from real competition: five national MMA titles, three Combat Sambo championships and medals at some of the sport’s most respected tournaments.

Whether you are stepping on the mat for the first time or preparing for your next fight, you train with someone who has done it at the top level.
